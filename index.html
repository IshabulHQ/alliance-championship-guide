Deploy buffed — once

Do this when you register

Registration is a snapshot. Your heroes, gear, troops and buffs at that exact moment are frozen for all 5 rounds. So buff first, register second — in that order.

  1. 1

    Turn your buffs on — gem buffs included

    Pop your gem buffs (the attack / defense buff items) and activate your pet combat buffs before you register. A good window is right after Bear Trap, while those buffs are already up — don't spend buffs just for AC if they're not already running.

  2. 2

    Check the Minister

    Register while a Minister buff is live if one is running. Sources don't agree whether Minister of Defense or Strategy is better — either live buff beats registering with none.

  3. 3

    Best heroes, best gear, your ratio

    Your strongest 3 PvP heroes with your best gear on them, Infantry hero as captain, and the troop ratio from your row in the table below. No Gina — she's a gathering hero.

  4. 4

    Register — then leave it alone

    Register once, while buffed. Then don't touch it. R4/R5 will move you between lanes during prep — that's their job, not yours.

⚠ The one mistake that costs people:

Re-registering after your buffs fade overwrites your strong buffed march with a weaker unbuffed one. If you already registered buffed, do not register again. If you left the alliance and came back during sign-up, your march was reset — you do need to register again, buffed.

How lanes are assigned

R4/R5 call

You don't pick your final lane — R4/R5 move people in prep, about 11 hours before each round. The plan is to stack our strongest ~40 across two lanes and concede the third: a 2–1 win counts exactly the same as 3–0. Only the top 20 in each lane actually fight, weakest first, max 2 fights per march — so depth matters, not just the strongest account.

Priority lane A

Stacked with anchors and depth. One of the two lanes we play to win.

Priority lane B

Stacked the same way. Winning A + B wins the round, whatever happens in the third.

Sacrifice lane

Conceded on purpose — but never left empty. An empty lane is an automatic loss, and even one member can steal a lane the enemy leaves empty. Which lane gets sacrificed rotates round to round.

Round 1 is blind — nobody sees the enemy's lanes. From Round 2 you see their previous round's setup, and they can rearrange, so R4/R5 work from last round's picture, not live intel.
